From pond to plate

Makhana Processing Cycle – From Bihar Pond to Plate

Harvesting from Ponds

Farmers collect seeds from lotus ponds in Bihar

→

Sun Drying & Sorting

Seeds are spread and dried under the warm sun

→

Boiling (Tempering)

Seeds are tempered in large pots over fire

→

Popping

Seeds pop into fluffy white foxnuts

→

Roasting & Flavoring

Seasoned with spices for delicious taste

→
