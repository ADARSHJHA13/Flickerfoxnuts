Our story

Built on Bihar roots. Driven by global trust.

Welcome to Flicker Global — an emerging Indian export company focused on supplying premium quality foxnuts (makhana) to global markets with transparency and reliable service.

01 · Roots

Deeply connected to Bihar

Although we are a new company in the export sector, our roots are deeply connected with the traditional foxnuts (makhana) farming ecosystem of Bihar, India — the world's largest makhana-producing region.

A part of our family has long been involved in farming and local sourcing activities, giving us practical knowledge of crop quality, grading, roasting standards and seasonal supply conditions.

02 · Quality

Strict checks, farm to dispatch

This direct ground-level understanding helps us maintain strict quality checks from sourcing to final dispatch.

At Flicker Global, we work closely with farmers, processors and local suppliers to source export-grade makhana with proper sorting, moisture control and hygienic packaging.

03 · Consistency

Built for international buyers

We understand that consistency, freshness and uniform grading are critical for international buyers, especially in repeat shipments.

  • Export-grade sorting
  • Moisture control
  • Hygienic packaging
04 · Trust

Win-win partnerships

We believe in building trust and long-term relationships with our business partners. At Flicker Global, trust can never be compromised.

We always aim to create a win-win business environment for both us and our customers through transparency, consistency and reliable service.

Export Terms

Exports can be arranged as per the buyer's required Incoterms, including FOB, CFR, CIF and Letter of Credit (LC) transactions.

  • FOB
  • CFR
  • CIF
  • Letter of Credit (LC)
⚓

Ports & Logistics

Our preferred export gateways are East Indian ports due to their proximity to the sourcing and manufacturing locations, which helps in maintaining product quality and operational efficiency.

However, shipments can be arranged from any Indian port depending on buyer requirements and logistics feasibility.

🔍

Surveyor Support

We also have independent surveyor support available at major Indian ports for export supervision and shipment coordination.

Additionally, we welcome buyer-appointed surveyors at loading ports to ensure complete transparency and confidence during cargo handling and dispatch procedures.

Makhana Processing Cycle – From Bihar Pond to Plate

Harvesting from Ponds

Farmers collect seeds from lotus ponds in Bihar

→

Sun Drying & Sorting

Seeds are spread and dried under the warm sun

→

Boiling (Tempering)

Seeds are tempered in large pots over fire

→

Popping

Seeds pop into fluffy white foxnuts

→
